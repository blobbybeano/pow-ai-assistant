📲 Add PowWash to your Home Screen

Add PowWash to your Home Screen

Follow these 3 steps in Safari:

  1. 1
    Tap the ⬆ Share button at the bottom of Safari (the box with an arrow pointing up)
  2. 2
    Scroll down in the menu that appears and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. 3
    Tap "Add" in the top right — PowWash will appear on your home screen like a normal app

⚠️ This only works in Safari. If you're in Chrome, copy the URL and open it in Safari first.
